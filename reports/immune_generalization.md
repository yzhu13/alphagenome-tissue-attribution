Generated by `scripts/step40b_immune_analysis.py` (loci: `step40_build_immune_loci.py`; scores: `step34_full_rescoring.py --variants data/immune_loci/immune_variants.csv --out data/immune_scoring`).

Immune lead variants scored: **1,154** of 1154 (parts read 12, unreadable 0). Calibration background: **2,000** random common null variants from the main run (parts read 22; unreadable main-run parts skipped: 0).

## Plain-English summary

- **The artifact generalizes.** With the original pipeline (raw scores pooled over DNase, ATAC and RNA), 1,154 lead variants for six immune-mediated diseases put **eye** first (DNase share of its tracks 100%; eye #1, immune #2 of 12). Random common variants give **eye** first under the same pipeline (eye #1), so the raw ranking reflects assay composition, not immune biology.
- **Calibrated but pooled across assays:** null-percentile scores pooled over all assays put **immune** first (immune #1, eye #12). Immune loci exceed the null more in RNA than in DNase, so pooling still favours RNA-rich tissues: among the non-immune tissues, Spearman(score, RNA share of tracks) = 0.95; here the immune signal is strong enough to stay on top, but the eye (DNase-only) drops to last.
- **Assay-matched calibration recovers the known answer.** Comparing DNase tracks only, immune ranks #1 (AlphaGenome |quantile|) and #1 (null percentile) of 12; bootstrap over loci: immune #1 in 83% / 74% of resamples.
- **Agreement with independent S-LDSC (6 traits, 11 categories):** mean Spearman 0.37 for raw pooled vs 0.27 (DNase |q|) and 0.22 (DNase null pct); higher in 2/6 and 2/6 traits (paired Wilcoxon p = 0.34 and 0.16; with 6 traits the smallest possible p is 0.031).
  Caveat: the reference has no eye category, so this benchmark (like step38) drops eye and cannot penalise the raw pipeline's eye artifact; with eye removed, raw pooled ranks the S-LDSC top category #1.0 (median over traits). Mean-tau* reference tops are not always immune (see table in d), and 11-category Spearman values are noisy.
