## d. Interpretation

- Matching works: the matched null has the GWAS loci's distance-to-TSS distribution (KS D = 0.008, vs 0.265 for the random null; median 41,880 vs 41,878 bp for GWAS and 130,824 bp for the random null).
- Gene proximity explains part of the RNA excess: random-null variants sit at mean RNA percentile 0.40 against the matched null (<0.5 = gene-proximal variants score higher in RNA). For GWAS loci (recommended scorers) the RNA − DNase gap is 0.104 against the random null and 0.025 against the matched null (76% smaller).
- With the matched null the gap is **largely gone**: the RNA > DNase excess mostly reflects that GWAS loci are closer to genes. Pooled calibrated rankings against a proximity-matched null are less confounded; the assay-matched comparison is still the conservative choice.
- Eye rank of 12 tissues. Raw pooled (original pipeline): eye #1 (95% 1-1), kidney #1 (95% 1-2), metabolic #1 (95% 1-1). Calibrated pooled, matched null: eye #1 (95% 1-3), kidney #12 (95% 12-12), metabolic #3 (95% 1-8). Calibrated DNase only, matched null: eye #1 (95% 1-2), kidney #3 (95% 1-6), metabolic #1 (95% 1-2) (random null: eye #2 (95% 1-4), kidney #6 (95% 4-8), metabolic #4 (95% 2-7)). DNase tissue means differ by only a few percentile points, so DNase ranks are unstable; read them with the bootstrap ranges.
- Provisional: based on 7,782 of 7,782 GWAS loci; rerun this script when the main run finishes.
